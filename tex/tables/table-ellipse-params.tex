$n_{\rm geo}$               & 2                  & Number of moment-based geometry-refinement iterations per object \\

$F_{\rm major}^{\rm final}$ & 0.1                & As above, applied when building the final mask after the geometry has converged \\
$f_{\rm ref}$               & 2.0                & Factor by which another SGA source's semi-major axis is extended when protecting its flux from masking \\
$\alpha_{\rm rad}$          & 0.7                & Exponent of the radial weight applied to the flux in the second-moment calculation \\
$f_{\rm sat}$               & 0.3                & Semi-major-axis ratio (relative to the largest overlapping neighbor) below which an object is flagged a satellite \\
$\Delta_{\rm max}$          & 3\farcs5           & Maximum allowed shift between the initial and moment-refined center before flagging an unreliable solution \\
$\mu_{\rm iso}$             & 22, 23, 24, 25, 26 & Surface-brightness thresholds (mag~arcsec$^{-2}$) for isophotal-radius measurements \\
$N_{\rm MC}$                & 50                 & Number of Monte Carlo realizations for isophotal-radius uncertainties \\
